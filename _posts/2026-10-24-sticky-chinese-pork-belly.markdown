Sticky Chinese belly pork — slow-cooked until meltingly tender, then finished in a sticky chilli glaze. Serves 6. From Nicky Corbishley at Kitchen Sanctuary.

**Ingredients**

*For the pork:*

* 1 kg (2.2 lbs) rindless pork belly slices, chopped in half (each piece approx. the length of your index finger)
* 1 litre (4¼ cups) hot chicken stock
* 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, peeled and minced (or 1 tbsp ginger puree)
* 3 cloves garlic, peeled and roughly chopped
* 1 tbsp rice wine
* 1 tbsp caster sugar

*For the glaze:*

* 2 tbsp vegetable oil
* Pinch of salt and pepper
* 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, peeled and minced
* 1 red chilli, finely chopped
* 2 tbsp honey
* 2 tbsp brown sugar
* 3 tbsp dark soy sauce
* 1 tsp lemongrass paste

*To serve:*

* Chopped spring onions
* Chopped red chillies

**Instructions**

1. Add the pork belly slices, stock, ginger, garlic, rice wine, and sugar to a heavy-based pan (a cast iron casserole pan works well).
2. Bring to the boil, then place a lid on, turn down the heat, and simmer for 2 hours.
3. Turn off the heat, remove the pork from the pan, and pat dry with kitchen towels. Reserve the liquid if you like — it's perfect for a Thai or Chinese noodle soup.
4. Chop the pork into bite-sized chunks.
5. In a small bowl, mix together 1 tbsp of the oil, a pinch of salt and pepper, ginger, chilli, honey, brown sugar, soy sauce, and lemongrass paste.
6. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil to a frying pan and heat over medium-high.
7. Add the pork along with a pinch of salt and pepper and fry, turning regularly, until it starts to turn golden. Be careful — the oil can splatter at this stage (a splatter guard helps).
8. Pour the glaze over the pork and continue to cook for a couple of minutes, turning often, until the pork looks dark and sticky.
9. Remove from the heat and serve, topped with spring onions and chopped chillies.

**Notes**

* Prep 10 min · Cook 2 hr 15 min · Total 2 hr 25 min.
* Freezes well — many cooks make a double or triple batch of the pork and reserve the cooking stock for other dishes.
* For a less fatty result, pork shoulder can be substituted for the belly.
