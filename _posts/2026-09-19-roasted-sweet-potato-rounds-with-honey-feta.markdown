Caramelized sweet potato rounds topped with crumbled feta, honey, and fresh herbs. A perfect side dish that works as an appetizer or light meal over arugula.

**Ingredients**

* 2 large sweet potatoes, cut into ½-inch thick rounds
* 3 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tsp smoked paprika
* ½ tsp ground cinnamon
* ½ tsp garlic powder
* ½ tsp salt, plus flaky sea salt for finishing
* ¼ tsp black pepper
* 4 oz (115g) feta cheese, crumbled
* 3 tbsp honey
* 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
* 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
* 2 tsp fresh lemon juice

**Instructions**

1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Season the sweet potatoes. Toss rounds with olive oil, smoked paprika, cinnamon, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
3. Arrange. Place rounds in a single layer on the baking sheet — no overlapping or they'll steam instead of roast.
4. Roast. Bake 15 minutes, flip each round, then roast another 12–15 minutes until tender inside with caramelized, slightly charred edges.
5. Add feta. Remove from oven and scatter crumbled feta over the warm rounds. Return to oven for 2–3 minutes just to soften the cheese slightly.
6. Finish. Transfer to a serving platter. Drizzle generously with honey and a squeeze of lemon juice. Scatter fresh parsley and thyme over the top. Finish with flaky sea salt and a crack of black pepper.
7. Serve warm as a side dish, appetizer, or over a bed of arugula for a light meal.

**Notes**

* Uniform thickness matters — uneven rounds cook at different rates and some will burn.
* Leave space between rounds for proper caramelization.
* Add honey after roasting — drizzling it before will burn and turn bitter.

**Variations**

* Swap feta for goat cheese or shaved Parmesan.
* Add a pinch of red pepper flakes to the seasoning for heat.
* Top with toasted pecans or pistachios for crunch.
