Homemade taco seasoning for seasoning ground meat. Much better than packets and fully customizable. Makes enough for one pound of ground meat per batch, or scale up for bulk preparation.

**Ingredients (1x — one pound ground meat)**

* 1 tablespoon chili powder
* 1 teaspoon ground cumin
* 1 teaspoon paprika (regular or smoked)
* ¾ to 1 teaspoon salt (kosher or sea salt)
* ½ teaspoon garlic powder
* ½ teaspoon onion powder
* ½ teaspoon dried oregano (crushed in your palm)
* ¼ teaspoon black pepper
* ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes or a pinch of cayenne (optional, for heat)

**Ingredients (4x — four pounds ground meat)**

* 4 tablespoons chili powder
* 4 teaspoons ground cumin
* 4 teaspoons paprika (regular or smoked)
* 3 to 4 teaspoons salt (kosher or sea salt)
* 2 teaspoons garlic powder
* 2 teaspoons onion powder
* 2 teaspoons dried oregano (crushed in your palm)
* 1 teaspoon black pepper
* 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes or a pinch of cayenne (optional, for heat)

**Instructions**

1. Combine all spices in a small bowl or jar.
2. Mix well to distribute evenly.
3. For immediate use: add to one pound of browned ground meat with 2–3 tablespoons water and simmer for 2–3 minutes until the meat is coated and flavors meld.
4. For bulk storage: keep in an airtight container in a cool, dark place for up to three months. Label with the date.

**Notes**

* Crush the oregano between your palms before adding to break it up and release the oils.
* The salt range (¾–1 tsp) depends on preference and whether your other ingredients are salted.
* Red pepper flakes/cayenne is optional; adjust to your heat preference.
* Store bulk batches in glass jars with airtight lids to preserve potency.
