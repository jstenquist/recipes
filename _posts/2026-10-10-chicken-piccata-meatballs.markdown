Tender ground-chicken meatballs browned and simmered in a bright, buttery lemon-caper piccata sauce. Serves 4 — about 40 minutes start to finish.

**Ingredients**

_For the meatballs_

* 1 lb (450 g) ground chicken
* 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
* 1 large egg
* 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
* 2 garlic cloves, minced
* 2 tbsp finely chopped parsley
* 1 tsp lemon zest
* 1 tbsp lemon juice
* 1/2 tsp kosher salt
* 1/4 tsp black pepper
* 2 tbsp olive oil, for cooking

_For the piccata sauce_

* 2 tbsp butter
* 2 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 1/4 cups chicken broth
* 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
* 2 tbsp capers, drained
* 1 tsp Dijon mustard
* 1 tbsp flour mixed with 2 tbsp water, or 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 1 tbsp water
* 2 tbsp chopped parsley
* 1–2 tbsp heavy cream (optional, for a silkier sauce)
* Salt and black pepper, to taste

_For serving_

* Lemon slices or wedges
* Extra parsley
* Mashed potatoes, pasta, rice, or roasted vegetables

**Instructions**

1. **Make the meatball mixture.** In a large bowl, combine ground chicken, panko, egg, Parmesan, garlic, parsley, lemon zest, lemon juice, salt, and black pepper. Mix just until combined. Form into 14–16 meatballs.
2. **Brown the meatballs.** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the meatballs and cook for 6–8 minutes, turning often, until golden on most sides. They do not need to be fully cooked yet. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Start the piccata sauce.** In the same skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Add garlic and cook for 20 seconds. Pour in chicken broth, lemon juice, capers, and Dijon mustard. Stir and bring to a gentle simmer.
4. **Thicken the sauce.** Stir in the flour slurry or cornstarch slurry. Simmer 1–2 minutes until slightly thickened and glossy. For a creamier look, stir in 1–2 tbsp heavy cream.
5. **Finish cooking.** Return the meatballs and any juices to the skillet. Spoon sauce over them, cover loosely, and simmer 6–8 minutes until the meatballs are cooked through and tender.
6. **Finish and serve.** Sprinkle with chopped parsley. Serve with lemon slices and extra sauce spooned over the top.

**Notes**

* Ground chicken is soft, so lightly oil your hands when rolling the meatballs.
* Don't overmix or the meatballs can turn dense.
* For a stronger piccata flavor, add a little extra lemon zest at the end.
* If you love capers, add an extra spoonful for more salty-briny punch.
* Optional add-ins: a handful of baby spinach stirred into the sauce, a little extra Parmesan in the meatball mix, or crushed red pepper for a subtle kick.
