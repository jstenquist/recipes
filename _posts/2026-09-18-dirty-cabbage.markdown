A Cajun one-pan skillet with fried cabbage, ground beef, and andouille sausage. Think dirty rice, but swap the rice for cabbage. Low-carb, high-protein comfort food packed with flavor.

**Ingredients**

* 1 medium head green cabbage (chopped, about 2 lbs)
* 1 lb ground beef
* 12-14 oz andouille sausage (diced)
* 1 onion (diced)
* 1 red bell pepper (diced)
* 1 green bell pepper (diced)
* 2 celery stalks (diced)
* 3-4 garlic cloves (minced)
* 1-2 tbsp Cajun seasoning
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 1 tsp smoked paprika
* Salt and black pepper (to taste)
* Oil (for cooking)
* Optional: green onions, hot sauce, splash of stock

**Instructions**

1. Crisp the diced andouille in a large skillet; remove and set aside.
2. Brown the ground beef in the same pan, season, and set aside. Leave a little fat.
3. Cook the onion, bell peppers, and celery until softened; add garlic and cook 1 minute.
4. Add cabbage, cumin, smoked paprika, and remaining Cajun seasoning. Cook down until wilted with some color, adding a splash of stock if needed.
5. Return beef and andouille; stir and cook a few minutes to marry the flavors.
6. Adjust seasoning, finish with green onions and hot sauce, and serve hot.

**Notes**

Serves 6. Total prep and cook time about 45 minutes (20 min prep, 25 min cook).
