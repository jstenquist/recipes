A quick weeknight pasta — tender chicken and sweet roasted red peppers in a creamy Parmesan sauce tossed with farfalle. Ready in 35 minutes, serves 4.

**Ingredients**

* 340 g farfalle pasta
* 450 g boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cubed
* 1–2 tbsp olive oil
* 3 garlic cloves, minced
* 1½ cups roasted red peppers, drained and sliced
* 1 cup heavy cream
* ¾ cup grated Parmesan cheese
* ½ tsp Italian seasoning
* ½ tsp smoked paprika (optional)
* Salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes to taste
* ½ cup reserved pasta water, as needed
* Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish

**Instructions**

1. **Cook pasta:** Boil farfalle in salted water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water, then drain.
2. **Cook chicken:** Season chicken with salt and pepper. Sear in olive oil over medium-high heat for 5–7 minutes, until cooked through (74°C/165°F). Set aside.
3. **Prepare sauce:** Sauté garlic for 30–60 seconds. Add roasted peppers, cream, Italian seasoning, and paprika. Simmer gently for 2–3 minutes.
4. **Add Parmesan:** Stir in Parmesan over low heat until smooth. Return chicken to the skillet.
5. **Combine:** Add pasta and toss to coat. Loosen the sauce with pasta water as needed. Adjust seasoning.
6. **Serve:** Garnish with parsley, extra Parmesan, and black pepper. Serve hot.

**Notes**

* Use freshly grated Parmesan for a smoother sauce.
* Add pasta water gradually for the ideal consistency.
* Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days; reheat gently with a splash of milk or cream.
