Comforting, savory, and slurpable — lean ground beef, slaw mix, and carrots cook in a simple tamari sauce in the crockpot, with ramen noodles stirred in at the end. Serves 6. From Real Food Whole Life.

**Tools**

* 6-quart slow cooker

**Ingredients**

* 1 lb lean ground beef
* 9 oz slaw mix (about 4 cups)
* 2 cups shredded carrots
* 3 garlic cloves, minced
* ⅓ cup low-sodium tamari (or soy sauce)
* 2 cups chicken stock
* 1 tsp brown sugar
* 2 (3-oz) packages ramen noodles, flavor packets discarded or saved for another use

**Instructions**

1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef, break it into small chunks, and cook until browned and no longer pink, about 5 minutes.
2. Transfer the beef to a 6-quart slow cooker. Add the slaw mix, carrots, garlic, tamari, stock, and brown sugar, and stir to combine.
3. Cover and cook on high for 2–3 hours or low for 4–6 hours, until the vegetables are tender and the sauce is bubbling. Don't lift the lid during this time.
4. Turn to high (if not already) and add the noodles, stirring to submerge them in the sauce. Cook on high for 5–15 minutes, until the noodles are just tender. Serve immediately with toppings.

**Notes**

* **More flavor:** Add any or all of these to the sauce: 2 tsp freshly grated ginger, 2 tbsp hoisin, 1–3 tsp sriracha, 1 tsp sesame oil, and bump the brown sugar up to 3 tbsp.
* **Toppings:** Sliced green onions, sesame seeds, sriracha, and/or chili crisp (highly recommended).
* **Chicken:** Be cautious subbing chicken — it releases a lot of liquid.
* **Gluten-free:** Use gluten-free tamari and rice ramen noodles; watch the noodles, as cooking time may vary.
* Prep 10 min · Cook 2–3 hr on high (or 4–6 hr on low).
